Quick 3-Step Setup

Step 1: Connect phone to laptop using a USB data cable.

Step 2: Enable USB Debugging in Settings > Developer Options.

Step 3: Double-click UsbScreenMirror.exe and click ▶ START SCREEN MIRROR & REMOTE CONTROL.
